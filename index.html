About

Product design leadership for the AI era.

S S Sashikiran is an AI Product Manager and UX Design Strategist based in Chennai, with over a decade of experience building the products, design systems, and AI workflows that decision-makers trust.

Sashikiran operates at the rare intersection of business understanding, design thinking, and engineering mindset. He specializes in translating dense domain complexity from enterprise analytics to high-velocity human experiences. Across his strategic roles at LatentView Analytics and prior design leadership at To The New, he has shaped experience strategies for global category leaders including Amazon Prime, SoftBank, Magnum, L&T, Tata Play, Bally's Interactive, and Denso across OTT, Ad-Tech, e-commerce and finance.

Beyond shipping enterprise-grade platforms, Sashikiran is actively defining the future of product craft in India by his 7S Design Framework. As an Executive MBA alumnus of IIM Raipur, his influence extends far past the interface and into the broader product ecosystem. Having mentored over 500+ product designers and reached more than 30,000 learners through workshops and webinars, he stands among the premier voices in Tamil Nadu and across the nation shaping not only how intelligent digital products are built today, but how the next generation of product and design leaders will think, build, and lead tomorrow.
